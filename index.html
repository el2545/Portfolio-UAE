05 - Methodology pipeline

Evidence before claims: a defensible evaluation sequence.

The methods section is intentionally procedural. Each step asks what must be true before the analysis can support a stronger claim.

1

Data gate

Why it matters: confirms intervention timing, geography, outcomes, controls, permissions and PDPL-aware handling.

2

Baseline construction

Why it matters: defines pre-intervention corridors, comparison areas, traffic indicators and QGIS/SUMO evidence boundaries.

3

Identification strategy

Why it matters: applies DiD and event-study logic only when treatment and comparison conditions are defensible.

4

Forecasting layer

Why it matters: uses forecasting or time-series models as predictive support, not a substitute for causal evidence.

5

Economic appraisal

Why it matters: translates estimated effects into CBA, ROI, NPV, IRR and welfare-accounting terms.

6

Robustness and limits

Why it matters: tests MCDA, BWM, TOPSIS-style weighting, carbon prices and downgrade rules.

7

Policy interpretation

Why it matters: reports only what the evidence supports for transport productivity, safety, emissions and governance.
